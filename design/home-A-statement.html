How it works

  1. You forward an invoicePDF, photo or email. Upload it or send it to Telegram.
  2. Two models read it privatelyBoth run inside NEAR AI's secure hardware and must agree on every figure.
  3. It's checked against the vendor's own recordThe vendor registered its payout address on-chain. Addresses printed on invoices are ignored.
  4. Your rules decideSmall, known invoices pay themselves. Anything unusual waits for your wallet signature.
  5. The vendor is paid their wayUSDC on NEAR, Base, Solana and more, settled through NEAR Intents in about 25 seconds.

For businesses

Set a monthly cap, a per-invoice limit and an auto-pay threshold. The AI holds a key that can only propose payments — it can't withdraw or change your rules.
